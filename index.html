A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Before you break bread.

How do I order in this preview?

Add menu items to your basket and choose a one-time, weekly, or monthly rhythm. Save your plan on this device or copy it to send to Krys through Instagram. Nothing is submitted automatically.

Where and when can I pick up?

Pickup location, bake days, and order cutoffs will be added once Krys confirms them. Coordinate directly before making a trip.

What about ingredients and allergies?

Full ingredient lists and kitchen cross-contact information have not yet been added to this site. Ask her directly about any dietary needs before ordering.

Can I order for a gathering or give bread as a gift?

Use the notes field in your order plan for gatherings, gift requests, or a special bake. Quantities and lead times should be agreed with Krys.

Can I change or pause a recurring order?

The bread club currently helps you plan a recurring request. Live subscriptions, pause controls, and payments can be connected when the bakery is ready.
